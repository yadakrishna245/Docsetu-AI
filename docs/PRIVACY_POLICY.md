## 1. Introduction

Welcome to DocSetu AI ("we," "us," "our," or "the Company"). DocSetu AI is an AI-powered document intelligence platform designed for Indian businesses and professionals. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you use our services.

This policy is drafted in compliance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023** of India and applies to all users accessing DocSetu AI through our website, application, or API.

By using our services, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

## 2. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of data:

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Storage Infrastructure
- **Documents**: Stored in AWS S3 with server-side encryption (AES-256) at rest
- **Metadata and account data**: Stored in AWS DynamoDB with encryption enabled
- **All infrastructure**: Hosted in AWS Mumbai region (ap-south-1)

### 4.2 Security Measures
- **Encryption at rest**: AES-256 for all stored documents and data
- **Encryption in transit**: TLS 1.2+ for all data transmission
- **Authentication**: JSON Web Token (JWT) based authentication
- **Access control**: Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) for all platform features
- **Audit logging**: All document access and modifications are logged
- **Regular security assessments**: Periodic vulnerability scanning and penetration testing

---

## 5. Data Retention

- **Active documents**: Retained while your account is active and for **90 days** after last access to the document
- **Inactive documents**: Automatically deleted 90 days after last access
- **Account deletion**: All documents, extracted text, and personal data are permanently deleted within 30 days of account deletion request
- **Billing records**: Retained for 7 years as required by Indian tax law
- **Aggregated analytics**: May be retained indefinitely in anonymized form

## 6. Third-Party Data Sharing

We share data with the following third-party processors, strictly for service delivery:

| Third Party | Data Shared | Purpose |
|-------------|-------------|---------|
| OpenAI / Google AI | Extracted document text (no personal identifiers) | LLM-powered document analysis and compliance checking |

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

## 8. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act 2023

As a Data Principal under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following rights:

### 8.1 Right to Access
You may request a summary of your personal data being processed, the processing activities performed, and the categories of third parties with whom data has been shared.

### 8.2 Right to Correction
You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data. You can update your account information directly from your dashboard.

### 8.3 Right to Erasure
You may request deletion of your personal data. Upon receiving a valid erasure request, we will delete your data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

### 8.4 Right to Grievance Redressal
You have the right to register a grievance regarding our data processing practices. We will acknowledge your grievance within 48 hours and resolve it within 30 days.

### 8.5 Right to Nominate
You may nominate another individual to exercise your data rights in case of your death or incapacity, as provided under the DPDP Act.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **grievance@docsetu.ai**.
